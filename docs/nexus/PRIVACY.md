# NEXUS — Privacy

- Camera and microphone **never activate silently**: explicit click + browser prompt + a live
  `MIC` / `CAM` indicator; switching off stops the hardware tracks.
- Tracking is on-device; frames are uploaded only when you ask a vision question.
- Voice recognition uses your browser's engine (Chrome/Edge process audio in their cloud) — see
  VOICE.md for the local Whisper path.
- Memory is fully visible and editable; can be disabled, exported or deleted.
- "Delete everything" (Security panel) removes profile, memories, tasks, permissions, rules,
  pending actions, feed and notifications.
- No biometric identification. Identity = your MATA account.
- NEXUS doesn't identify people in camera images.
