# NEXUS — Shopping

**Status: planned (Phase 14).** Comparing products through web research works today.

Rules (already enforced by the confirmation engine): `shopping_purchase` is HIGH risk, always
requires confirmation, and **can never be covered by a trusted automation rule**. Before any
purchase NEXUS must show product, price, seller, shipping, tax (when available), total and return
policy. Planned integrations: eBay Browse API, Shopify Storefront, Amazon PA-API (approval needed).
